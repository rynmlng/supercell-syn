## Chase Target Decision

### Scenario:
A large QLCS/MCS is advancing southeast across the northern Plains (MT→ND→SD). Discrete cells are initiating along the dry line at ~-99°W in south-central SD by early-mid afternoon (18–20Z). These SD cells have minimal surface-based CAPE (260 J/kg) and are likely elevated/forced by the larger system. However, the warm sector ahead of the dry line in central Nebraska features extreme instability (CAPE 4383 J/kg) and shear (SRH 142/360 m²/s²), with STP 2.5 indicating significant tornado potential.
